About Me

I'm a final-year Computer Science student at Capital University of Science & Technology (CUST) in Islamabad, graduating in 2026 with a specialisation in cybersecurity. I hold an ISC² CC certification and am actively working toward my CEH.

My internship at Corvit Networks Pvt Ltd gave me real-world exposure to vulnerability assessments, log analysis, incident response workflows, and client-facing security documentation — the kind of work I'm looking to continue and grow in.

Before moving fully into security, I worked in two onsite roles that sharpened my professional edge significantly. At Servixer LLC, I worked as a Transcriber — handling audio files and converting them into accurate, formatted documents under tight deadlines. I started as a rookie and worked alongside veteran transcribers, consistently meeting my quota and earning a monthly performance bonus within the first three months — one of the few rookies recognised for working at full-team pace. At Mighty Knights, I worked as a Customer Service Representative on live campaigns, handling customers from the United States across different industries including Medicare insurance sales. I dealt with real people, real objections, and complex product knowledge — developing strong communication, active listening, de-escalation, and persuasion skills that are directly valuable in client-facing and high-pressure security environments.

I scored in the 91st percentile nationally on the HEC NCST, reflecting both breadth and depth of technical foundation. I'm meticulous, composed under pressure, and focused on outcomes — not just tools.

Currently targeting SOC Analyst, Red Team, and IT Security roles in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and remote positions with international exposure.

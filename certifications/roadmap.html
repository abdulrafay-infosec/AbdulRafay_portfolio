Strategy

A deliberate progression from entry-level penetration testing credentials through defensive fundamentals and blue-team skills, culminating in OSCP — the industry benchmark for practical offensive security.

01

eJPT — Junior Penetration Tester

Entry-level practical pentest cert. Validates recon, scanning, exploitation basics. Target: Q3 2026.

02

CompTIA Security+

Broad defensive foundation — network security, compliance, IR. Complements SOC Analyst positioning. Target: Q4 2026.

03

BTL1 — Blue Team Level 1

Hands-on defensive operations — log analysis, threat hunting, SIEM. Strengthens blue-team credibility. Target: 2027.

04

OSCP — Offensive Security Certified Professional

Advanced 24-hour practical exam. The long-term goal for offensive security depth. Target: 2027–2028.
